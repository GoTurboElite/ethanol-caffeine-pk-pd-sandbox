## Numerical contract

`science/metrics.mjs` constructs B − A at the sorted union of dense time knots over shared coverage. Each trajectory uses its own piecewise-linear interpolation. Integrals use exact window clipping and compensated trapezoidal sums. Signed ΔAUC equals B AUC minus A AUC within floating-point tolerance. Absolute divergence splits every interior sign-changing segment at its linear root and integrates the two nonnegative triangles. The canonical integral units are g·h/dL and mg·h/L.

The earliest occurrence represents each tied extremum. Positive and negative extrema are absent when their signs are absent. Strict sign reversals through a zero plateau carry its start and end times. Touching zero or reaching it at a window boundary is recorded separately. An identical pair has zero signed and absolute areas, no signed extrema and no sign reversals. Clipping a window never extrapolates or resolves an open threshold return. Threshold durations are directly differenced at equal reference levels.

## State and identity decisions

- **Main scenario:** existing committed draft and accepted snapshot contracts remain authoritative. Experiment edits do not replace main inputs. Global calculation feedback on the experimental route is labelled Main scenario.
- **Experimental reference:** one active, recursively frozen record captures the accepted canonical scenario, full input key, compact display identity, originating snapshot, behavioural profile, model ID and release fingerprint. The display checksum is not a cryptographic authenticity claim. Full keys establish correspondence; profile and constants are retained. Reference replacement is explicit.
- **Experimental draft:** a separate validated scenario supports registered scalar/enum/boolean physiological and PK parameters, constants, intake amount/timing and meal schedules. Unapplied form entries remain local and visibly flagged; calculation, application and saving are blocked until resolved. The committed experimental draft is in history and portable sessions.
- **Calculated experiment:** existing runtime comparison jobs compute both sides. Single-use request IDs, serial checks and captured keys prevent superseded results replacing newer outputs. Previous outputs retain their captured record when draft inputs change or a job fails. Runtime owners pin at most the active calculated pair; replacement/closing releases their ownership.
- **Apply/restore:** restore copies the original reference into the experimental draft. Apply requires a matching calculated experiment and uses one atomic validated main-scenario history entry. It replaces the complete main scenario; Undo restores prior inputs. Ordinary Undo/Redo can recover matching local computations; imported sessions/experiment files invalidate transient computations and require recalculation.
- **Sweeps:** worker jobs, with a cooperative fallback, operate on a captured draft. One axis supports 2–21 values; two axes are capped at 49 combinations. A nominal 2.5-million-step work budget is checked before execution. Cancellation, progress and source keys protect promotion. Members retain inputs/metrics, not dozens of dense trajectories. Promotion creates an uncalculated explicit experimental draft.
- **Presentation:** plot windows, axes and cursors are local view state and do not enter numerical/scientific input identities. Analytical-view exports record them alongside captured identity and derived metrics. Reduced chart paths never supply exposure integration or fitting residuals.

Session wrapper **1.2.0** adds the bounded nullable experiment record. Scientific document **1.2.0** adds nullable dataset context. Versions 1.0/1.1 migrate with empty new fields; historical conversion factors/notes and numerical observations remain preserved. Unknown future versions, malformed reference identities, invalid scenarios and inconsistent dataset packages are rejected before replacement. Experiment schema and dataset-package schema are separately versioned **1.0.0**. The 8 MiB session and existing history/archive bounds remain. Large histories may require the existing history-free export path.

## Analytical definitions and plotting

`science/metrics.mjs` clips dense trajectories at exact selected boundaries using piecewise-linear interpolation. AUC uses compensated trapezoidal summation in g·h/dL and mg·h/L. The engine-refined global peak is retained when inside an interval; other extrema use dense samples. No extrapolation is performed. Comparisons report signed and percentage differences over the common interval; percentages are undefined for zero references. Separate full-horizon AUC and threshold status remain visible when horizons differ. Each trajectory can also be inspected over its own complete horizon.

Threshold summaries retain original crossings, complete exceedance episodes and right-censoring. Window intersections label zero-duration boundary contacts separately from sustained intervals. Global final returns remain unresolved when the calculation ends with an open episode; window clipping does not manufacture a resolved return.

`ui/analytical-plot.jsx` is shared by Analysis, legacy A/B comparison, experiments, observational evaluation/calibration and uncertainty. Controls support numeric bounds, zoom buttons, pan slider, absorption/peak/elimination presets, threshold-episode selection and full-horizon reset. Automatic axes follow visible positive values; fixed axes retain joint full-horizon bounds; custom nonnegative bounds explicitly report clipping. Each analyte retains its own units and compared trajectories share that analyte's axis. Overlay scales remain independently labelled. Tick generation uses finite 1/2/5 steps with scientific notation where appropriate.

Reference and experimental lines use distinct stroke patterns, with numerical values and signed differences at the same time. SVG sliders, native numeric/range controls, keyboard navigation, horizontal pointer capture and touch-intent handling provide alternative interactions. Vertical touch scrolling remains available. A smaller view window adds no solver resolution. Scientific evaluation and uncertainty use their captured reduced display grids; cursor interpolation is labelled accordingly. Their residuals and per-draw summaries are computed before reduction. Pointwise P50 curves are not individual trajectories, and their maxima are not medians of per-draw peaks.

Default keeps numerical machinery in optional disclosures. Detailed exposes units, relationships, windows and sweeps; Expert additionally exposes provenance, dense tables and support diagnostics. All three depths share calculations and remain independent of explanation/density/appearance/accessibility preferences. Inputs, BAC rings and the principal preview retain their prior experience. Legacy comparison and sensitivity bookmarks remain available; the integrated experimental route is linked from Analysis, Compare and scientific search.

## Sensitivity, residuals, fitting and sampling

Parameter sweeps report exact direct evaluations and finite-range secant effects with parameter/output units. Two-axis rectangular response tables show every value and a corner difference-in-differences contrast. These depend on the selected ranges; they are not local derivatives, uncertainty intervals or evidence of causal biological interactions. Threshold discontinuities and open returns remain explicit.

Residuals use predicted minus observed concentration. Signed, absolute and standardised measurement residuals are defined for quantified rows; upper limits retain one-sided exceedance penalties and are not plotted as measured signed residuals. Diagnostics separate calibration and held-out roles, analytes, supplied/default error scales and matrices. Time trends and concentration-dependent absolute residual trends are descriptive. Sparse rows, repeated cohort means and heterogeneous errors limit inferential interpretation. Observation marks distinguish roles and censoring, with supplied uncertainty bars and complete numerical alternatives.

Calibration retains the optimiser, objective and eligibility checks. New diagnostics expose actual finite-difference values, weighted log-parameter sensitivity norms, column dependence, boundary distance, search/diagnostic simulation counts and one-parameter objective support. A 7×7 two-parameter conditional objective surface is not a nuisance-optimised profile or confidence region. Coarse delta-SSE support is conditional on independent supplied Gaussian errors; it is withheld for dataset-context group summaries and flagged when grid boundaries truncate support. Convergence, identifiability and predictive accuracy remain distinct. Eligible fits can be inspected through an unapplied experiment before the existing explicit fit application.

Uncertainty preserves RNG/distributions, Gaussian-copula dependence, rejection and absolute-draw pairing. Per-draw AUC augments peak/time summaries. Paired pointwise and outcome differences use B−A within the same draw, not subtraction of marginal quantiles. Equal horizons and selected baseline parameters remain required; unsupported pairs are rejected explicitly. The active experimental reference/draft supplies A/B when present, otherwise legacy A/B is used. Captured bounds, distributions, seed, sample count and dependence are inspectable/exported. Quantiles represent the specified parameter uncertainty; they do not add observation error or model-form uncertainty.

## External evidence and scientific limitations

The built-in trajectory is Tanaka et al. (2014), DOI [10.1248/bpb.b13-00401](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bpb/37/1/37_b13-00401/_html/-char/en), Figure 3: nine repeated plasma caffeine group means from four healthy men aged 34–41 after oral 100 mg caffeine in a five-probe cocktail. The numeric facts/SEM are from the [PK-DB curator CSV preview](https://testing.sysmo-db.org/data_files/542). The curator endpoint was not retrievable through the release browser research tool; its indexed CSV preview supplied the values. The primary article independently verified sampling times, population, dosing and the stated 8 h mean. Values were not independently redigitised from the figure. This access/provenance limitation is retained rather than implying a fresh raw-data download.

Original ng/mL means and digitised SEM are preserved. SEM is a declared descriptive error scale, not individual assay precision. The digitised 8 h value 2470.59 ng/mL is approximately 0.38% below the article's stated 2480 ng/mL. Predose concentration is retained; reconstruction initial central caffeine matches it. That row is marked calibration/initialisation, with the eight postdose rows reserved for evaluation; it is not counted as an independent held-out prediction. Unreported body mass, food and duration are explicit assumptions (70 kg, reference light-meal settings and instantaneous oral input); 5 h half-life/distribution are retained priors. Other probe drugs and interactions are not modelled. Under this reconstruction all eight postdose means are underpredicted: signed bias −1.08248 mg/L and RMSE 1.13074 mg/L. The predose anchor is reported separately. Reproduce the exact values with scripts/verify-study-reconstruction.mjs; docs/verification/v2.3-published-evaluation.json retains the full reconstruction, residuals and numeric-data checksum. Thus discrepancies are conditional on this reconstruction and cannot isolate structural model bias or establish individual predictive validity. Nine time points are not nine independent participants.

Dataset-package import validates source/population/administration/error/preprocessing metadata, row identity and reconstructed scenario before review. Confirmation replaces scenario and observations together as one Undo operation. Context is bound to the imported observations; later edits label it historical. Synthetic diagnostics remain explicitly synthetic and clear active external context. Existing four-summary benchmarks remain descriptive checks, separate from the additional trajectory. Broader candidate studies are documented in the 2022 [PK-DB caffeine analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2021.752826/full); further curation requires verified conditions and source access.

No independently supported model revision was established in this release. The engine, reference equations, constants and defaults remain **0.4.4 / 0.3.0 / reference-1.60**. The original prior-evidence registry remains **2026-10-28.1**; the additional evaluation dataset catalog is versioned separately **2026-11-02.1**. Scientific-workbench computation identity is **1.1.0**, analytical workbench **1.0.0**, and scenario schema remains **0.8.2**. Historical results retain their originating metadata and imported archives remain ineligible until recomputed.

`test/analytical-browser.mjs` adds complete three-archetype workflows, time/axis inspection, one/two-axis sweeps, application/restoration, intake edits, portable experiments, external review, calibration support, paired uncertainty, narrow/enlarged keyboard operation, actual v2.1.3 export migration, invalid unapplied fields and dense-plot measurements. CI requires these in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit alongside every retained suite, offline checks and exact hosted preview verification. Main repeats hosted checks on both production hostnames, followed by exact asset checks. Final pass/fail outcomes, commit/fingerprint and hosted evidence are reported by the completed release workflow and engineering report rather than assumed here.

An exploratory local Chromium measurement for 72 h/100 intakes produced 17,281 points in 1.74 s; window selection plus ten cursor movements took 217 ms, with zero numerical reruns and at most 242 displayed vertices. JSON records include engine/machine context. These are scripted single-environment measurements, not a cross-device performance guarantee or human usability study. Final CI artifacts retain their own measurements.

Manual requirements remain: screen-reader task execution and announcement quality, physical iOS/Android gestures/installation, browser/OS zoom beyond app text enlargement, and observed comprehension at all three depths. Automated axe scans, emulated touch and scripted keyboard tasks do not complete those assessments. Larger data collections, alternative sampling schemes for unequal parameter baselines, nuisance-optimised multidimensional profiles and broader independently reconstructed validation remain future work.
